## The approach: completeness, not minimalism

One page, no whitespace wasted. The image is a dense one-day digest in the
broadsheet tradition:

- a **top bar** with the day's real counts (35 stories · 8 regions · 14 fields),
- a red **sub-headline** naming the three biggest stories,
- **THE DAY IN UTC** — a timeline of the day as it unfolded,
- a **statistics strip** (six key numbers of the day),
- **three lead stories** with drawn illustrations,
- **ten more numbered stories** in a two-column grid,
- a dotted **WHERE IT HAPPENED** world map with numbered pins matching stories 1–13,
- an **ALSO ON TUESDAY 24 NOVEMBER** small-print list with 22 further stories,
- and a **DATE CHECK** box at the foot explaining which tempting stories were
  left out because they belonged to Thursday, Monday or Sunday — not Tuesday.

Every story happened, or was first reported, on Tuesday 24 November 2026, and
every one is sourced below. Claims are labelled as claims (`OFFICIAL`, `REPORTED`,
`CLAIM`); nothing is invented.

## How it works

- `main.py` — the contest entry program (Python 3.13, standard library only).
  It verifies `entry.png` (PNG, ≤ 1 MiB, ≤ 1024×1024) and copies it to
  `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` (default `/output`). Two runs always produce identical bytes.
- `render.py` — the renderer that produced `entry.png` (needs Pillow and the
  Liberation fonts). It draws everything — masthead, charts, bridge and market
  illustrations, the dotted world map — from the sourced facts baked into it.
- `entry.png` — the rendered page: 1024×1024 PNG, ~238 KB.

Run the entry locally:

```sh
MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=./out python3 main.py
```
